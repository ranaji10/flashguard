Reference · why the scripts look awkward

They query an explicit allowlist of device properties one at a time instead of dumping everything. Both adb devices and fastboot getvar all print the device serial, and this dataset is published openly. If you ever see a serial, IMEI, MAC or Android ID in output you are about to record, that is a defect in the scripts worth reporting, not something to quietly edit out.
